MHW Prop Mail Assistant

A private tool used by its owner to read and send their own Google Mail and Drive.

What this application does

MHW Prop Mail Assistant is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It connects to the owner's own Google account so that the owner can search, read, draft and send their own email, and search and open their own Google Drive files, from an AI assistant interface instead of the Gmail website.

Who it is for

This is not a public product. It is used only by the individual who set it up, on their own Google accounts. There is no sign-up, no user base, and nothing is offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Gmail — reading, searching, labelling, drafting and sending messages in the owner's own mailbox.
  • Google Drive — searching, reading and creating files in the owner's own Drive.

It accesses only the account that granted it permission, and only while the owner is running it.
